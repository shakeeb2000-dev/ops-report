Settings

New report

Clears everything on screen so you can start again. Your name, site and trigger levels are kept, and reports you have already saved are not touched.

Backup

Everything is kept on this phone only. If it is lost or reset, your reports go with it — so save a backup now and again, somewhere off the phone such as your email or Google Drive. Photos are not included.

Using two devices? Load the other one’s backup here to bring them together. Readings and dosing times from both are combined; where the same morning exists on both, the one saved later is kept.

 

App version

The app keeps its own copy on this phone, so it opens with no signal. It only needs signal to get the app the first time, and to pick up a new version after you upload one.

If you have just uploaded an update and the version below has not changed, tap Check for an update — the phone can hold on to the old copy otherwise.

Open it from the app’s icon on your Home screen. An address typed into Chrome without the final / cannot use the saved copy, and will ask the internet instead.

Protected from phone clean-ups: if this says no, the phone may delete the app’s saved copy — and your readings — when it runs short of space. Adding the app to your Home screen (Chrome menu, then Install app) protects them. Save a backup either way.

Trigger levels

The numbers that decide when a check is flagged. At or past Fault it is reported as a fault; at or past Monitor, as needing a watch. The four chemicals are judged on how many days they will last. Their litre levels are only used until the app has enough history to know how fast each one goes.

Average usage

How far back to look when working out average daily use. Reports older than this are ignored. Because a report can cover several days — a weekend or a few days off — the average may end up covering slightly more days than this number.
Advanced
Saved reports, how the figures are worked out, and repairs. Not needed day to day.

Saved reports

Where the average flow comes from

The water the meter recorded, divided by the hours, over past days when the dosing ran right through from one meter reading to the next. Only those days can be measured exactly, so only those are used. Your Flow rate check is not part of this — it is reported as you type it.

Every dosing entry

The full list, newest first. An extra entry in the middle of a day stops that day counting towards the average flow above. Remove any wrong ones from the Chemical dosing card on the main screen.

Deleting every entry and reopening the app brings back the seven times recorded on paper.

Recorded readings and reports

Puts back everything written down before the app existed — the meter readings and the reports for 11 Sep to 2 Oct 2026. Use this if the average flow looks wrong, or if the panel above says meter reading missing for any of those days. Anything saved for those dates is replaced; other dates are untouched.
