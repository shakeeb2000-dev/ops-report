Settings

New report

Clears everything on screen so you can start again. Your name, site and trigger levels are kept, and reports you have already saved are not touched.

Trigger levels

Set the numbers that decide when an item is flagged. Anything at or past the Fault figure is reported as a fault; anything at or past Monitor is reported as needing a watch.

Average usage

How far back to look when working out average daily use. Reports older than this are ignored. Because a report can cover several days — a weekend or a few days off — the average may end up covering slightly more days than this number.

Backup & moving between devices

Each device keeps its own copy — your phone and your PC do not talk to each other. Save a backup file here and load it on the other device to bring them together. Readings and dosing times from both are combined; where the same morning exists on both, the one saved later is kept. Photos are not included.

This is also your only backup. Save one now and again — if the phone is lost or cleared, everything goes with it.

App version

Running version dev. If the version here does not match what you uploaded, tap below — the phone can hold on to the old copy otherwise.

Where the water speed comes from

The speed is averaged over past days where the dosing ran right through the whole period between two meter readings. Only those days can be measured exactly, so only those are used.

Every dosing entry

The full list, newest first. An extra entry in the middle of a day stops that day counting towards the speed above. Remove any wrong ones from the Chemical dosing card on the main screen.

Deleting every entry and reopening the app brings back the seven times recorded on paper.

Recorded readings and reports

Puts back everything written down before the app existed — the meter readings and the reports for 11 Sep to 2 Oct 2026. Use this if the water speed looks wrong, or if the panel above says meter reading missing for any of those days. Anything saved for those dates is replaced; other dates are untouched.
